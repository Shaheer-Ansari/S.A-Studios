How a project moves.

The same four stages, whether it's a five-minute reel or a full brand package.

01

Ingest & select

Reviewing raw footage or assets and marking the moments worth building around.

02

Structure the cut

A rough assembly focused on pacing and story before any polish goes in.

03

Finish

Color, sound design, graphics, and retouching layered on top of a locked cut.

04

Deliver

Exports formatted for wherever it's going — platform, aspect ratio, and spec handled up front.
